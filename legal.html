Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

StreamVault is a static website. It runs no user database and collects no personal data on any server. In short: your data stays in your browser.

1. What is stored, and where

  • Local storage (your browser only): optional account profile (name, e-mail, salted SHA-256 password hash, role), watchlist, playback positions, UI settings (language, maturity level, motion, quality), admin event log.
  • Session storage: a short-lived cache of TMDB API responses (30 minutes) to reduce network traffic.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers, no fingerprinting.

You can erase everything at once via Settings → Clear local data, or by clearing site data in your browser.

2. Third parties the site calls

  • TMDB — catalogue data and images. Requests include your chosen interface language; see TMDB’s privacy policy.
  • Internet Archive — metadata for public-domain films when you press play.
  • VidRift — video embed provider for non-public-domain titles; their player may apply its own logging when loaded. See vidrift.net.
  • Disify — optional e-mail syntax/disposability check when you submit the newsletter form. Degrades to a local regex check if unreachable.
  • JustWatch (via TMDB) — “Where to watch” provider links.

3. Passwords

Local account passwords are never stored in plain text — only a salted SHA-256 hash (sha256('sv1:' + email + ':' + password)) kept in your own browser. Because accounts are local, losing your browser data means losing the account.

4. Children

The Service includes a maturity filter (see the Content Policy) but is not directed at children. Use parental controls available on your device to restrict access where required.

Privacy questions: message the operator on Discord.

Content Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

StreamVault curates its catalogue automatically from TMDB. The following rules define what appears on the Service.

1. Excluded content — LGBT-themed titles

Titles whose theme is LGBT (lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer and closely related keyword categories) are excluded from the catalogue by operator policy. They are filtered from browsing (all rows, the library and every tab — Movies, TV and Anime) and are additionally blocked at playback time, including on the standalone player page.

2. Maturity ratings

The Service applies a default maturity ceiling of Teen (max PG-13 / TV-14). Titles rated R, NC-17 or TV-MA are hidden unless the viewer raises the level to Mature in Settings → Content maturity. Available levels:

  • All ages — only G / TV-G.
  • Family — up to PG / TV-PG.
  • Teen (default) — up to PG-13 / TV-14.
  • Mature — no ceiling (R / NC-17 / TV-MA appear).

Selecting an exact rating in the library’s Age Rating filter is a deliberate choice by the viewer and overrides the ceiling for that search. Certifications come from TMDB (US certification data); coverage is not guaranteed for every title.

3. Other exclusions

  • Explicit adult/pornographic material is never included (TMDB adult content is requested with include_adult=false everywhere).
  • Genre tiles, rows and the Top-10 list all inherit the same filters — there is no unfiltered surface.

4. Reports

If you believe a title is miscategorised and should be excluded (or restored), report it on Discord. Verified reports are actioned, typically within 7 days.

DMCA Notice & Takedown Procedure

Last updated: September 23, 2026

StreamVault does not host any video content. Playback is performed by third parties (the Internet Archive for verified public-domain films, and independent embed providers for other titles). We respond to valid copyright notices promptly.

1. Designated agent

Copyright notices go to the operator: Dev-zwoz on Discord (fastest) or via the GitHub repository issues page.

2. Filing a notice

A valid takedown notice must include:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed;
  2. The exact URL(s) on StreamVault where the allegedly infringing title appears;
  3. Your full name, address, e-mail and, if you are an entity, your company details;
  4. A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner;
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

3. What happens next

  • Valid notices are actioned typically within 7 business days: the title is removed from the catalogue and, where technically possible, from play resolution.
  • We forward a copy of the notice to the embed provider actually hosting the file when appropriate.
  • Incomplete notices delay processing until the missing information arrives.

4. Counter-notice

If content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notice containing the same elements above plus consent to jurisdiction of your local courts. We will restore the entry unless the original complainant files a court action within 10 business days.

5. Repeat infringers

Because StreamVault hosts no files and no uploads, repeat-infringer policies apply to the underlying hosts (Internet Archive, embed providers), not to this Service.
